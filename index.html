01 / ABOUT

A mathematician working at the intersection of PDE control theory and functional analysis.

My research focuses on controllability problems for distributed parameter systems and evolution equations. I combine analytical tools including Carleman estimates, semigroup theory, observability inequalities, delay systems, and fractional calculus to investigate exact and approximate controllability of fractional PDEs and nonlinear systems with memory and nonlocal conditions.

I am currently a PhD candidate (CGPA 8.7/10, expected completion December 2026) in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology (IIST), Thiruvananthapuram, supervised by Prof. Raju K. George. Alongside the analytical work, I'm increasingly interested in how neural ODEs and operator-learning ideas can approximate the trajectories of controlled dynamical systems.

04 / PROJECTS

Current lines of investigation.

01
Delay and Memory-Type Null Controllability
Establishing null controllability results for parabolic and heat-type equations subject to delay effects and memory kernels, in both finite and infinite dimensions.
02
Carleman Estimates with Moving Controls
Developing geometric characterizations and Carleman-estimate-based observability results for systems actuated through time-varying (moving) control regions.
03
Conformable Fractional PDE Systems
Existence, uniqueness, and exact/approximate controllability of non-autonomous semi-linear systems governed by conformable and Caputo fractional derivatives.
04
Degenerate Parabolic Equations
Controllability of non-autonomous degenerate parabolic equations with boundary degeneracy and time-dependent diffusion coefficients.
05
Fractional Evolution Systems with Nonlocal Conditions
An operator-theoretic framework for optimal control and approximate controllability of nonlinear fractional evolution equations subject to nonlocal initial conditions.
06
Semiautonomous Neural ODEs
Using neural ordinary differential equations to approximate the trajectories of controlled nonlinear dynamical systems and C₀-semigroups.
