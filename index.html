01About

An applied mathematician at the interface of control, dynamical systems, PDEs and scientific machine learning.

My research centres on observability and null controllability of parabolic systems with delay and memory, Carleman estimates, and the controllability of degenerate and non-autonomous PDEs. In parallel, I study approximation, controllability and robustness of semi-autonomous neural ordinary differential equations — connecting control-theoretic guarantees with learning-based models.

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Mathematics at IIST Thiruvananthapuram under Prof. Raju K. George (CGPA 8.7/10, expected completion January 2027), and hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics from IIT Jodhpur. I have served as a teaching assistant for Foundations of Machine Learning, Calculus, and Linear Algebra & Calculus of Variations.

02Research

Two threads, one question: can we steer it?

Thread A · Control of PDEs

Observability & null controllability

∂ty − Δy + ∫0t k(t−s) y(s) ds = χω u find u so that y(T) = 0

Parabolic systems with delay and memory, moving control regions, and degenerate or non-autonomous coefficients. The toolkit: Carleman estimates, observability inequalities, functional analysis and operator theory.

  • Memory can obstruct null controllability — two dichotomies for linear systems
  • Geometric characterisation with moving controls
  • Divergence and non-divergence degenerate parabolic equations
Thread B · Neural dynamical systems

Controlled semi-autonomous neural ODEs

ẋ = f(x) + Σi gi(x) ui  ≈  neural ODE control-affine dynamics, schematic

Neural ODEs as universal approximators of controlled nonlinear dynamics — with guarantees. The questions: how well can the trajectories be approximated, can the learned model still be controlled, and is it robust?

  • Approximation & controllability of nonlinear control-affine systems
  • Turnpike-preserving universal approximation
  • Robustness and numerical evaluation
